# Shipping

## Delivery times

Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days within the EU. Express delivery takes 1 business day to most EU addresses and must be selected at checkout.

## Costs

Standard delivery is free on orders over 50 EUR, and 4.90 EUR otherwise. Express delivery costs 12.90 EUR regardless of order value.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking link by email when it ships. Order status moves from placed, to shipped, to delivered. If an order shows as shipped for more than 7 business days without a delivery, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

## Changing or cancelling an order

An order can be changed or cancelled free of charge while its status is placed. Once it has shipped it can no longer be changed; return it after delivery instead.

## Where we ship

We ship to all EU countries, Switzerland, Norway and the United Kingdom. Deliveries outside the EU may be subject to import duties, which are paid by the recipient.
