# Returns and refunds

## Return window

You can return any order within 30 days of delivery. The window starts on the delivery date shown in your order history, not the order date. Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be returned; if you no longer want an order that is still in transit, refuse the delivery or return it once it arrives.

## What can be returned

Returns cover the whole order. Products must be complete, with all accessories and cables, and in their original packaging where possible. Opened products are accepted within the window as long as they are undamaged. Keyboards with custom keycaps or switches fitted by you must be returned with the original parts reinstalled.

## How to return

Open a return request through support with your order number and the email address you ordered with. You will receive a return ID and a prepaid shipping label by email within one business day. Pack the items, attach the label and drop the parcel at any carrier point within 14 days of opening the request.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the parcel arriving at our warehouse. The refund covers the full price of the returned products. Original shipping costs are refunded only when the return is due to a defect or an error on our side. Your bank may take a further 3 to 5 business days to show the refund.

## After the window

Once the 30 day window has closed, products can no longer be returned for a refund. Faulty products are still covered by the warranty.
