# Warranty

## Coverage

All products carry a 2 year warranty from the delivery date, covering manufacturing defects and failures under normal use. Keyboards and mice also carry an additional 1 year switch warranty, for 3 years in total, against switch failure and key chatter.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear such as shine on keycaps or worn mouse feet, or damage caused by modification. Batteries are covered for 1 year.

## Making a claim

Contact support with your order number, the email address you ordered with, and a short description of the fault. A photo or short video helps. If the fault is confirmed we send a replacement; if the product is no longer available we refund the price paid. Shipping costs for warranty claims are always covered by us.

## Faulty on arrival

A product that is faulty on arrival can be returned within the 30 day return window for a full refund including shipping, or replaced under warranty, whichever you prefer.
